# MarsLogistics OS - AI Tracking Engine Architecture

## Technical Summary of Domain Contracts

The domain model defines the core structures for a multimodal tracking engine equipped with predictive AI capabilities to assess operational risk, detect silent delays, and optimize cargo flow.

### 1. Shipment
The core root entity representing multimodal freight in transit.
- **Responsibilities**: Holds origin/destination routing, current status (`PENDING`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `HELD`, `EXCEPTION`, `DELIVERED`), multimodal transport mode (`SEA`, `ROAD`, `AIR`, `RAIL`, `MULTIMODAL`), primary carrier assignment, and AI assessment references.

### 2. MilestoneEvent
A chronological telemetry entry denoting an event in the shipment lifecycle.
- **Responsibilities**: Contains timestamp, operational checkpoint name, location, normalized status (`PENDING`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `FAILED`), carrier name, and raw telemetry descriptions (`rawStatusDescription`) to form the Smart Multimodal Timeline.

### 3. Document
Represents trade, customs, and transport compliance files attached to a shipment.
- **Responsibilities**: Tracks regulatory documents (`COMMERCIAL_INVOICE`, `CUSTOMS_DECLARATION`, `BILL_OF_LADING`, `PACKING_LIST`) and verification states (`VERIFIED`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `REJECTED`).

### 4. AiAssessment
Evaluative output generated by the AI Exception Triage Engine.
- **Responsibilities**: Exposes risk classification (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), confidence score, deviation metrics (`isDelayed`, `predictedDelayHours`), ETA reconciliation (`confirmedEta` vs. `predictedEta`), diagnostic summary, and executable action proposals (`recommendedAction`).

---

## Role Flows

### OPERATIONS
Logistics controllers manage portfolio exceptions and execute automated mitigations.
- **Exception Triage**: Real-time filtering by risk level (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`) and held shipments (`HELD`), enabling fast intervention on customs holds and stale telemetry.
- **AI Next Action**: Executes `POST /api/v1/shipments/{id}/triage` to approve assisted resolution payloads (pre-filled customs emails, carrier escalations), transitioning status to `IN_TRANSIT`.
- **Incident Resolution**: Submits manual intervention audits via `POST /api/v1/shipments/{id}/resolve` when custom handling is required.

### CUSTOMER
End clients access a read-optimized self-service view focused on transparency.
- **Self-Service Portal**: Direct tracking access via `GET /api/v1/shipments/{id}` without requiring operational intervention.
- **Smart Multimodal Timeline**: Visual progression across multimodal legs, surfacing original telemetry and milestone confirmations.
- **Proactive Delay Alerts**: Early warning banners derived from AI ETA divergence, notifying customers before carrier delivery deadlines are breached.
